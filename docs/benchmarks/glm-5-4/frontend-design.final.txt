## The five takes

One product across all five — a second-brain notes app called **Slip** — so you're comparing directions, not different briefs. Each direction comes from the note-taking world rather than a generic SaaS kit:

| Route | Name | Idea | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/1` | **Slip box** | Manila index cards (Zettelkasten IDs) on an oxblood leather desk. Hover a card and red threads run to the cards it links to. | Courier Prime (typewriter) |
| `/2` | **Legal pad** | The page *is* a canary legal pad with real rules and a red margin. The CTA is an unticked checkbox that opens the sign-up form. | Shantell Sans (hand-drawn) |
| `/3` | **Margins** | A book page with highlighter marks connected by pencil lines to margin notes; colours mean something (quote / question / idea). Hover to follow a thread. | Newsreader (book serif) |
| `/4` | **Chalkboard** | A chalk-drawn graph of notes that draws itself in on load; hovering a dot lights up its links. Features sit on a chalk tray. | Rubik |
| `/5` | **Two brains** | Pure black/white split with type that inverts across the line. **Drag the divider** (or arrow keys) — the Ebbinghaus forgetting curve snaps to a flat line wherever you drop it. | Archivo (variable width) |

## The switcher

A small pill at the bottom-right (`▤ 2 / 5`) opens a panel listing all five takes with a colour swatch, marks the one you're on, and closes on Escape, outside click, or navigation. Bonus: press **1–5** on any page to jump.
