Built five responsive landing pages for **Morrow**, a second-brain note-taking app. The preview is running on **port 3432**, and the **Design** button in the bottom-right corner switches between all five.

- [**/1 · Signal**](http://localhost:3432/1) — Luminous artwork and floating notes show how ideas connect.
- [**/2 · Field Notes**](http://localhost:3432/2) — A scrapbook-inspired design for collecting observations.
- [**/3 · Atlas**](http://localhost:3432/3) — A clickable map lets you follow connections between notes.
- [**/4 · After Hours**](http://localhost:3432/4) — A dark, search-first design for finding half-remembered ideas.
- [**/5 · Commonplace**](http://localhost:3432/5) — A library preview lets you browse sample collections.

The home page opens Signal. Demo notes are saved in your browser and can be found from any of the five designs.

Verified all five pages at six screen widths, including keyboard navigation for the switcher. The production build and lint checks passed.
