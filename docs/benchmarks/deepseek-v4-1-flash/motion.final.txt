## 1 — "Constellation" (dark, cinematic)
A starfield in deep indigo. Twelve note cards drift in from the dark, links draw themselves between them with travelling light pulses, and the cards condense into a spinning ring of orbiting thoughts. Beats: **Fragments** → **Connected** → **Recall**, then everything collapses into a single point and blooms into the logo.

- Real 3D camera with a bezier-interpolated dolly path, depth-of-field fade, atmospheric dust
- Curved link threads that draw along their own arc, plus a three-node triangle motif that plants the logo before the reveal
- Serif italic + Inter, warm amber on cool violet

## 2 — "Commonplace" (warm, tactile, editorial)
A blank index card on textured cream paper. Text writes itself on with a wipe, the camera pulls back to reveal nine more cards flying in from off-frame with real velocity-derived motion blur, ink links spider between them. Then the camera pushes back into the hero card, a highlighter swipe lands, and the mark resolves.

- Procedural paper grain, fibres, and a soft light shaft
- Hand-drawn ink rules and an underline using value noise, not straight lines
- Every note is real written-on-card content, not lorem

## 3 — "Signal" (Swiss, kinetic type, product UI)
Off-white and ink. **THINK IN LINKS.** slams in line by line with a lime highlighter strike, dissolves; a knowledge graph assembles node by node with a live link counter; then the actual app UI rises — sidebar, breadcrumbs, note body, backlinks panel — before resolving to the wordmark.

- Per-line type wipes with a clip-rect reveal, not fades
- A real designed app surface, not a mockup approximation
- Deliberate restraint: no fast whips, everything eased
