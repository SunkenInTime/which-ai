I have designed five distinct landing page iterations for **Noema**, a "Second Brain" note-taking application, accessible at `/1`, `/2`, `/3`, `/4`, and `/5` (running on `http://localhost:3281`, with `/` redirecting to `/1`), complete with a persistent floating **Iteration Switcher** in the bottom-right corner.

---

### Floating Iteration Switcher (`app/components/IterationSwitcher.tsx`)
A persistent floating pill dock sits in the bottom-right corner across every page (`app/layout.tsx`):
- **One-Click Route Pills (`1` `2` `3` `4` `5`)**: Jump directly to `/1`, `/2`, `/3`, `/4`, or `/5`.
- **`Switch →` Cycle Button**: Cycles sequentially through the 5 iterations (`/1 → /2 → /3 → /4 → /5 → /1`).
- **Expandable Iteration Menu**: Clicking the active iteration name opens a popover overview of all 5 design directions.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**: Press `[` or `]` to step backward/forward between iterations, or `Alt+1` through `Alt+5`.

---

### The 5 Second-Brain Landing Page Iterations

#### 1. `/1` — **The Neural Observatory** (`app/1/page.tsx`)
*Bioluminescent Dark Graph, Bidirectional `[[Wikilinks]]` & Local Vector Recall*
- **Aesthetic & Typography**: Deep obsidian canvas (`#06080E`) with cyan (`#38BDF8`), violet (`#A78BFA`), and amber (`#F59E0B`) accents, pairing `Space Grotesk`, `Instrument Serif`, and `JetBrains Mono`.
- **Interactive Features**:
  - **Live SVG Synaptic Knowledge Graph + Bidirectional Note Workbench**: Click any node in the graph or any `[[Wikilink]]` pill inside the note body to traverse synapses, inspect bidirectional backlinks, and view emergent synthesis bridges.
  - **Live Thought Injection Bar**: Type a thought in the hero input to dynamically inject a new linked node directly into the graph.
  - **Semantic Threshold Slider & Folder-vs-Graph Toggle** + **`⌘K` Command Modal**.

#### 2. `/2` — **The Scholar's Atelier** (`app/2/page.tsx`)
*Warm Archival Paper, Luhmann Zettelkasten Slip-Box & Side-by-Side Marginalia*
- **Aesthetic & Typography**: Warm vellum parchment (`#FAF6EE`), iron-gall ink (`#191613`), terracotta vermilion (`#C84B31`), and botanical sage (`#285948`), pairing `Newsreader`, `Lora`, and `IBM Plex Mono`.
- **Interactive Features**:
  - **Interactive Folgezettel Slip-Box Desk**: Switch between numbered alphanumeric index cards (`21a1`, `21a2`, `21a2a`, `21b`), inspect the linked primary literature source on the left, follow branching Folgezettel trails, or write new marginalia to mint a new numbered slip-card on the desk.
  - **Interactive 4-Stage Progressive Distillation Stepper**: Step through *I. Literature Stream*, *II. Marginalia*, *III. Atomic Zettel Distillation*, and *IV. Manuscript Assembly*.

#### 3. `/3` — **Swiss Cognitive OS** (`app/3/page.tsx`)
*High-Craft Brutalist Grid, Sub-Millisecond Command Engine & FSRS-5 Memory Drills*
- **Aesthetic & Typography**: Industrial Swiss daylight (`#F4F4F0` & `#FFFFFF`) with exposed `1px` `#0A0A0A` structural grid hairlines and international orange (`#FF3B00`) / cobalt (`#0047FF`) accents, pairing `Space Grotesk` and `JetBrains Mono`.
- **Interactive Features**:
  - **Multi-Mode Command Workbench**:
    - **`[⌘K] Instant Recall`**: Live filterable search across indexed technical notes with AST inspector.
    - **`[⌘J] Interstitial Log`**: Enter timestamped thoughts and watch automatic `@person`, `#project`, and `[[Node]]` extraction.
    - **`[⌘R] FSRS-5 Cloze Drill`**: Interactive spaced-repetition cloze flashcard extracted directly from markdown notes.
  - **Interactive Ergonomic Key-Chord Matrix** (`⌘ + K`, `⌘ + J`, `⌘ + \`, `⌘ + ⇧ + R`).

#### 4. `/4` — **The Spatial Canvas** (`app/4/page.tsx`)
*Infinite 2D Mind-Palace, Multimodal Cards & Spatial-to-Linear Manuscript Compiler*
- **Aesthetic & Typography**: Dark charcoal dot-grid studio (`#111318`) with floating cards and Bezier connector cables in purple (`#C084FC`), mint (`#34D399`), coral (`#FB7185`), and amber (`#FBBF24`), pairing `Bricolage Grotesque` and `JetBrains Mono`.
- **Interactive Features**:
  - **Interactive 2D Spatial Board**: Select any card on the canvas, switch spatial layouts (*Mind Palace*, *Theme Clusters*, *Linear Story Arc*), nudge card coordinates (`←` `↑` `↓` `→`), toggle the voice memo waveform player, or pin new sticky cards onto the board.
  - **One-Click Spatial → Linear Manuscript Transformer**: Toggle between the 2D spatial board and a compiled linear Markdown manuscript.

#### 5. `/5` — **The Ambient Companion** (`app/5/page.tsx`)
*Biophilic Calm, Zero-Maintenance Organization & Conversational Memory Recall*
